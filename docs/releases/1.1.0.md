# Verity 1.1.0 — release notes (draft, not tagged)

The owner tags and publishes; until then this describes the `[Unreleased]` section of
`CHANGELOG.md`. Figures below are copied from the committed artefacts named next to
them; the README and the system card render the same figures from those files.

## What changes for a reader of the numbers

- **A held-out test set.** 95 labelled questions over a 21-document corpus (Markdown,
  PDF, DOCX), split by a fixed seed into dev (39) and test (56). Thresholds were set on
  dev; the published scorecard is the test run, `eval_969e1d5_test.json`.
- **Every proportion with its interval.** On the test run
  (`scorecards/live-v1.1.0.json`): refusal recall 17/20, Wilson 95 % [0.640, 0.948];
  refusal precision 17/22, [0.566, 0.899]; 3 of 20 out-of-scope questions answered;
  faithfulness per claim 108/109 and citation accuracy per claim 105/109, both judged.
  Claims of one answer are correlated, so the per-claim intervals are too narrow.
- **A validated judge, with its limits.** The judge of the runs (judge prompt v0.1.0)
  agrees with blind human labels on 60 claims with κ 0.71; on real claims alone κ is
  0.48 and fragile (one unsupported claim in 40). An adjudication with the judge's
  verdict visible is published apart and does not replace the blind result.
- **A defended judge prompt, measured, not used by the runs.** Judge prompt v0.2.0
  delimits and datamarks its inputs and names the citation of each claim, checked in
  code (`citation_ok` is false without a valid index). Its rejudge stopped at its
  $1.30 cap after 52 of 60 claims: κ 0.60, against 0.64 for v0.1.0 on the same 52, a
  one-claim difference. No run of record has been judged by v0.2.0, and no adversarial
  eval of the judge exists.
- **Datamarked synthesis (from main, #34).** `synthesize.prompt` v0.3.0. Its injection
  measurement, made on the earlier 6-document corpus, steered no case in either arm
  (0/12, [0, 0.243]) and does not show an effect of the mark.

## What the numbers do not say

- Both v1.1.0 runs used `synthesize.prompt` v0.2.0 and judge prompt v0.1.0: they
  predate the datamarked synthesis and the defended judge.
- The confidence score is the scorer model's self-report; its calibration is not
  shown (C21, roadmap).
- Latency is one wall-clock per answer; the runs predate per-stage timings.

## Upgrading

- The Anthropic SDK is 2.x and no sampling parameter is sent (API version
  2026-10-21). A pinned 1.x environment needs `uv sync`.
- Eval run artefacts gain fields; files written before them load and resave byte for
  byte.
- The headline scorecard schema is v5; the system card generator refuses older ones.
