# FY2025 Operating Budget - Memo to the Board

From: Office of the Chief Financial Officer. Date: March 12, 2025. Subject: proposed operating budget for fiscal year 2025, for approval at the March 20 Board meeting.

## 1. Summary

The proposed operating expense budget for FY2025 is $268 million, an increase of 12% over FY2024 actual operating expenses of $239 million. Capital expenditure is budgeted at $34 million, mainly for data-center capacity in Frankfurt and Virginia.

## 2. Operating Expenses by Function

- Research and development: $112 million.
- Sales and marketing: $98 million.
- General and administrative: $58 million.

## 3. Headcount

The plan adds 220 net new employees over the year, 60% of them in research and development. Most hires are planned for the second half of the year.

## 4. Cost Measures

The company-wide travel budget is reduced by 10% compared to FY2024. External consulting spend is capped at $9 million. Any new spend above the approved budget for a function requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## 5. Risks

The budget assumes revenue growth at the midpoint of guidance, about 15.5%. If growth falls below 14%, the hiring plan for the second half will be reduced by up to half.
