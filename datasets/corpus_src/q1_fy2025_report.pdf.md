# Acme Corporation - Quarterly Report Q1 FY2025

Quarter ended March 31, 2025. Prepared for the Board of Directors, dated April 24, 2025. All figures in United States dollars and unaudited.

## Highlights

Total revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2025 was $128.9 million, an increase of 17.2% compared to $110.0 million in the first quarter of fiscal year 2024. Growth was led by Enterprise Cloud, which added 41 new enterprise customers in the quarter.

## Revenue by Segment

- Enterprise Cloud subscription revenue: $86.4 million, up 22.5% year over year.
- Managed Services revenue: $32.1 million, up 9.6% year over year.
- Other revenue: $10.4 million, down 1.9% year over year.

## Profitability

Gross margin was 72.6%, compared to 70.9% in the same quarter last year. Operating income was $21.3 million. Operating expenses grew more slowly than revenue because hiring was concentrated in the second half of the fiscal year.

## Cash

Cash and cash equivalents were $231.8 million at the end of the quarter. Operating cash flow for the quarter was $26.7 million. The share repurchase program approved in March had not started by the end of the quarter.

## Customers and People

Net revenue retention for Enterprise Cloud was 118%. Headcount at the end of the quarter was 1,940 employees.

## Outlook

Management reaffirms its fiscal year 2025 guidance of revenue growth between 14% and 17%. Guidance for the second quarter is revenue between $132 million and $135 million.
