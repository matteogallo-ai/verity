# Leave Policy

This policy describes the types of leave available to employees of Acme Corporation in addition to the paid time off described in the Employee Handbook. Effective January 1, 2025.

## 1. Parental Leave

A primary caregiver receives sixteen (16) weeks of fully paid parental leave. A secondary caregiver receives eight (8) weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within twelve (12) months of the birth, adoption or foster placement, and is available after three (3) months of employment.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive ten (10) days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over to the following year. A medical certificate is required for an absence of more than three (3) consecutive working days.

## 3. Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to five (5) days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member, and up to two (2) days for an extended family member.

## 4. Jury Duty

Employees called for jury duty receive full pay for up to ten (10) working days per year.

## 5. Sabbatical

After five (5) years of continuous employment, employees may take a paid sabbatical of four (4) weeks. A sabbatical must be requested at least three (3) months in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

## 6. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to three (3) months may be granted at the company's discretion. Health insurance continues during approved unpaid leave.
