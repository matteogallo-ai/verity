# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy owner: Finance. Version 3.2, effective January 1, 2025. This policy applies to all employees of Acme Corporation who travel or incur expenses on company business.

## 1. Approval

All business travel must be approved in advance by the employee's manager through the travel booking tool. International travel also requires approval from a Vice President.

## 2. Air Travel

Employees book economy class for flights scheduled at six (6) hours or less. Business class is allowed for flights scheduled at more than six (6) hours, with the approval of a Vice President. Tickets should be booked at least fourteen (14) days before departure when possible.

## 3. Hotels

The nightly hotel rate, excluding taxes, may not exceed US$250. In New York, London and San Francisco the limit is US$325 per night. Employees must use the preferred hotels listed in the booking tool when one is available.

## 4. Meals

Employees receive a meal per diem of US$75 per full travel day, and US$40 on the days of departure and return. The per diem replaces individual meal receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except during client entertainment approved in advance by a Director.

## 5. Ground Transportation

Employees should use public transport or taxis. Rental cars are allowed when cheaper than the alternatives or when traveling with colleagues. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at US$0.67 per mile.

## 6. Receipts and Submission

An itemized receipt is required for every expense of US$25 or more. Expense claims must be submitted within thirty (30) days after the trip ends. Claims submitted more than ninety (90) days after the trip are not reimbursed.

## 7. Non-reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed: upgrades paid for personally, airline lounge memberships, traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, and expenses for a spouse or family member.

## 8. Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than four (4) times per year are issued a corporate card. Personal expenses must never be charged to it.
