# Statement of Work No. 1 - Data Migration

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated January 15, 2024 between Acme Corporation ("Provider") and Northwind Logistics Inc. ("Customer"). It describes the migration of Customer's shipment-tracking data from its legacy on-premises system to Enterprise Cloud.

## 1. Scope

Provider will extract, transform and load approximately 40 million shipment records and 1.2 terabytes of attached documents from Customer's legacy Oracle database into Enterprise Cloud, and will validate record counts and checksums after each load.

## 2. Out of Scope

Migration of archived records older than seven (7) years, changes to Customer's internal reporting tools, and end-user training are not included in this SOW.

## 3. Timeline

The project runs for twelve (12) weeks starting March 3, 2025. The cutover to production is planned for the weekend of May 24, 2025.

## 4. Fees and Milestones

The fixed fee for this SOW is US$85,000, invoiced on completion of three milestones:

- Milestone 1, approved migration plan: 30% of the fee.
- Milestone 2, successful test migration in the staging environment: 40% of the fee.
- Milestone 3, production cutover accepted by Customer: 30% of the fee.

Travel expenses are billed separately at cost and may not exceed US$6,000 without Customer's written approval.

## 5. Acceptance

Customer has ten (10) business days after each milestone delivery to accept it or to describe in writing how it does not meet this SOW. A milestone not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

## 6. Change Requests

Any change to scope, timeline or fees requires a written change request signed by both parties. Provider will estimate the impact of a requested change within five (5) business days.
