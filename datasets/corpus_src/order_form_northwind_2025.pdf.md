# Order Form No. NW-2025-001

This Order Form is entered into under the Master Services Agreement dated January 15, 2024 between Acme Corporation ("Provider") and Northwind Logistics Inc., a Delaware corporation headquartered in Columbus, Ohio ("Customer"). In case of conflict, this Order Form prevails over the Master Services Agreement for the services listed below.

## 1. Subscription

- Service: Enterprise Cloud, Professional edition.
- Quantity: 250 named user seats.
- Price: US$42.00 per seat per month.
- Annual subscription fee: US$126,000.00.
- Subscription term: twenty-four (24) months, starting February 1, 2025 and ending January 31, 2027.

## 2. Add-on Services

- Managed Services, Standard tier: US$18,000.00 per year.
- Premium Support: included at no additional charge for the subscription term.

## 3. Billing and Payment

Fees are invoiced annually in advance on the subscription start date and on each anniversary. Notwithstanding Section 2 of the Master Services Agreement, Customer shall pay each invoice within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date. All amounts are exclusive of taxes.

## 4. Data Residency

Customer data for this subscription is hosted exclusively in Provider's Frankfurt, Germany data center. Provider will not move Customer data to another region without Customer's prior written consent.

## 5. Premium Support

Premium Support shortens the Priority 1 response time to thirty (30) minutes, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. All other support response times follow the Service Level Agreement. Customer is assigned a named technical account manager.

## 6. Seat Changes

Customer may add seats at any time at the same per-seat price, prorated to the end of the current annual period. Seats may be reduced only at an annual renewal date, and never below 200 seats during the subscription term.

## 7. Renewal

This Order Form renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term. Renewal pricing may increase by no more than five percent (5%) per year.

Signed for Acme Corporation by Maria Chen, Vice President of Sales, on January 20, 2025. Signed for Northwind Logistics Inc. by Tom Becker, Chief Operating Officer, on January 22, 2025.
