# Amendment No. 1 to the Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") amends the Master Services Agreement dated January 15, 2024 (the "Agreement") between Acme Corporation ("Provider") and Northwind Logistics Inc. ("Customer"). It is effective as of July 1, 2025. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement.

## 1. Limitation of Liability

Section 3.1 of the Agreement is amended so that, for Customer only, the aggregate liability of Provider shall not exceed two million United States dollars ($2,000,000) or the total fees paid by Customer during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim, whichever is greater.

## 2. Termination for Convenience

Section 1.2 of the Agreement is amended to require at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice for termination for convenience, instead of thirty (30) days. The pro-rata refund of prepaid fees is unchanged.

## 3. New Section 7 - AI Features

Provider shall not use Customer Data, including prompts and outputs of any AI-assisted feature, to train or fine-tune machine-learning models, whether its own or a third party's. AI-assisted features are disabled by default and are enabled only by a Customer administrator.

## 4. No Other Changes

Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. This Amendment does not change the Service Level Agreement or the Data Processing Addendum.
