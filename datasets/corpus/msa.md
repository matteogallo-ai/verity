# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 between Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and the Customer identified in the applicable Order Form ("Customer").

## 1. Term and Termination

### 1.1 Initial Term

The Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section 1.

### 1.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for its convenience by providing the other party with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Fees paid in advance for services not yet delivered will be refunded on a pro-rata basis, and outstanding fees for services rendered through the effective date of termination remain due and payable.

### 1.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after receiving written notice of the breach.

## 2. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

## 3. Liability and Indemnification

### 3.1 Limitation of Liability

The aggregate liability of Provider arising out of or related to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed one million United States dollars ($1,000,000) or the total fees paid by Customer to Provider under the applicable Order Form during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim, whichever is greater.

### 3.2 Data-Processing Breach

Acme Corporation, acting as Provider, shall be the entity liable for any breach of its data-processing obligations under Schedule B (Data Processing Addendum), subject to the cap in Section 3.1. Provider shall notify Customer of any confirmed data-processing breach within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery and shall reasonably cooperate with Customer's investigation.

### 3.3 Exclusion of Damages

Except for a party's indemnification obligations and breaches of confidentiality, neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits or loss of business, arising out of or related to this Agreement.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to protect the Confidential Information of the other party with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 5. Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other party; provided, however, that either party may assign this Agreement without such consent to a successor in interest in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, other than to a direct competitor of the non-assigning party. Any assignment in violation of this Section is null and void. In particular, the Agreement does not permit assignment to a competitor of the non-assigning party without such party's prior written consent.

## 6. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.
