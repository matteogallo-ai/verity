# Schedule B — Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the Master Services Agreement between Acme Corporation ("Provider", acting as data processor) and the Customer ("Controller"). It governs the processing of personal data on the Customer's behalf.

## 1. Roles and Scope
Provider processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller and solely to deliver the services described in the Order Form.

## 2. Sub-processors
Provider engages a cloud infrastructure provider and a transactional email provider as sub-processors. Provider will give the Controller at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor, during which the Controller may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

## 3. Data Location and International Transfers
Personal data is stored in data centers located in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union) and in Virginia, United States. Transfers of personal data from the EU to the US are carried out under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 4. Security Measures
Provider encrypts personal data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel on a least-privilege basis.

## 5. Breach Notification
Provider will notify the Controller of any confirmed personal-data breach within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery, and will cooperate with the Controller's investigation and remediation.

## 6. Data Subject Requests
Provider will assist the Controller in responding to data-subject requests (access, rectification, erasure) and will forward any such request it receives directly to the Controller within five (5) business days.

## 7. Retention and Deletion
Upon termination of the Agreement, Provider will delete all personal data processed on the Controller's behalf within ninety (90) days, unless retention is required by applicable law.

## 8. Audit
The Controller may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA once per calendar year, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice.
