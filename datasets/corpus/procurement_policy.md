# Procurement Policy

Policy owner: Finance. Effective January 1, 2025. This policy governs the purchase of goods and services by Acme Corporation.

## 1. Approval Thresholds

Every purchase requires a purchase order approved according to its total contract value:

- below US$10,000: the requester's manager;
- from US$10,000 to US$100,000: a Director and the Finance business partner;
- above US$100,000 and up to US$500,000: the Chief Financial Officer;
- above US$500,000: the Chief Executive Officer, after review by the Chief Financial Officer.

## 2. Competitive Quotes

Purchases above US$50,000 require at least three (3) written quotes, unless the supplier is the only one able to provide the goods or services. A sole-source justification must then be attached to the purchase order.

## 3. Vendor Onboarding

New suppliers are onboarded by the Procurement team. A supplier that will access company or customer data must also pass the security review described in the Information Security Policy before the contract is signed.

## 4. Payment Terms

Acme's standard payment term for suppliers is forty-five (45) days from the invoice date. Shorter terms require approval from the Finance business partner.

## 5. Splitting Purchases

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.
