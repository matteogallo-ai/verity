# Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to every customer and user of Acme Corporation's Enterprise Cloud service and forms part of the Master Services Agreement.

## 1. Prohibited Uses

Customers may not use the service to:

- store or transmit malware, or attempt to gain unauthorized access to any system;
- send unsolicited bulk email or messages;
- store payment card numbers outside the dedicated tokenization vault;
- mine cryptocurrency or run other workloads unrelated to the customer's business use of the service;
- perform load or penetration testing without Provider's written authorization, requested at least five (5) business days in advance.

## 2. API Rate Limits

Each tenant may send up to 1,000 API requests per minute. Requests above that limit receive HTTP status 429 and must be retried with exponential backoff. Higher limits are available on request for Professional and Enterprise editions.

## 3. Storage Quota

Each tenant includes 2 terabytes of document storage. Additional storage is billed at US$20 per terabyte per month.

## 4. Enforcement

Provider may suspend the affected part of the service after giving twenty-four (24) hours' written notice of a violation that the customer has not remedied. Provider may suspend without notice only when a violation poses an immediate threat to the security or availability of the service, and will restore it as soon as the threat is removed.

## 5. Reporting

Suspected violations should be reported to abuse@acme.example. Provider acknowledges reports within one (1) business day.
