# Code of Business Conduct

Acme Corporation expects every employee, officer and director to act with integrity. This Code is approved by the Board of Directors and applies worldwide.

## 1. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from business partners only when the value does not exceed US$100 per person per year and the gift is not cash or a cash equivalent. Any gift above that value must be declined or reported to the Compliance team. Gifts may never be offered to government officials without prior approval from Legal.

## 2. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose to Compliance any financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor, and any outside employment, before it begins. Employees may not take part in a decision that involves a company in which they or a family member hold an interest.

## 3. Insider Trading

Employees who hold material non-public information about Acme or another company may not trade in that company's securities, or advise others to do so.

## 4. Speaking Up

Employees can report suspected misconduct to their manager, to Compliance, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline, which is operated by an independent provider and available 24 hours a day. Acme does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## 5. Consequences

Violations of this Code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
