# Benefits Summary 2025

This summary describes the benefits offered to full-time employees of Acme Corporation in the United States for the 2025 plan year. The plan documents govern in case of any difference.

## Retirement

Acme matches 100% of employee contributions to the 401(k) plan up to 4% of base salary. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## Health Insurance

Acme pays 90% of the medical, dental and vision premiums for employees and 75% of the premiums for their dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

## Life and Disability Insurance

Acme provides life insurance of two times base salary and long-term disability insurance at no cost to the employee.

## Learning and Wellness

Each employee has a learning budget of US$1,500 per calendar year for courses, conferences and books, and a wellness allowance of US$600 per year. Unused amounts do not carry over.

## Employee Stock Purchase Plan

Employees may buy Acme shares at a 15% discount through the Employee Stock Purchase Plan, in two offering periods per year.
