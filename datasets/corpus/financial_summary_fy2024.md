# Acme Corporation — Financial Summary FY2024

*Prepared for internal review, dated March 5, 2025. All figures in United States dollars unless otherwise noted.*

## Executive summary

Acme Corporation reported a strong FY2024, with double-digit revenue growth driven by expansion of the Enterprise Cloud segment and continued adoption of the Managed Services offering.

## Revenue

Total revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $482.0 million, an increase of 18.4% compared to $407.1 million in fiscal year 2023. The year-over-year growth was propelled by:

- Enterprise Cloud subscription revenue of $312.7 million (up 24.1% YoY).
- Managed Services revenue of $128.6 million (up 11.2% YoY).
- Other revenue of $40.7 million, roughly flat versus the prior year.

The revenue mix continues to shift toward recurring subscription revenue, which represented 84.6% of total revenue in FY2024, compared to 79.3% in FY2023.

## Profitability

Gross margin expanded to 71.8% in FY2024 from 68.9% in FY2023, primarily reflecting scale in cloud infrastructure and improved contract terms with third-party data-center providers. Operating income was $71.4 million, up from $46.8 million in the prior year.

## Cash and liquidity

Cash and cash equivalents at fiscal year-end were $214.3 million, with total available liquidity — including undrawn credit facilities — of $364.3 million. Operating cash flow for the year was $92.5 million.

## Outlook

For fiscal year 2025, management expects revenue growth in the range of 14% to 17%, with continued gross-margin expansion of approximately 100 basis points. These figures are forward-looking and subject to the risk factors disclosed in Section 8 of the Annual Report.
