# Remote Work Policy

This policy complements the hybrid working model in the Employee Handbook. It is owned by People Operations and effective January 1, 2025.

## 1. Eligibility for Fully Remote Work

Employees may work fully remotely only with the written approval of a Vice President and People Operations. Fully remote work is not available during the probationary period. Approved remote employees are expected on site for one team week per quarter.

## 2. Home Office Support

Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of US$500 and a monthly internet allowance of US$50. Company equipment, including the laptop and one external monitor, remains company property and must be returned at the end of employment.

## 3. Working from Another Country

Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to thirty (30) days per calendar year, after notifying People Operations at least two (2) weeks in advance. Longer stays require approval from Legal because of tax and immigration rules.

## 4. Security

Remote work must follow the Information Security Policy. Restricted data may not be accessed over public Wi-Fi without the company VPN.
