# Service Level Agreement

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") applies to the services provided by Acme Corporation under the Master Services Agreement.

## 1. Uptime Commitment
Provider commits to a monthly uptime of 99.9% for the production service. Uptime is measured per calendar month and excludes scheduled maintenance windows.

## 2. Service Credits
If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the Customer is entitled to a service credit calculated as a percentage of that month's fees:

- Uptime from 99.0% up to 99.9%: 10% credit.
- Uptime from 95.0% up to 99.0%: 25% credit.
- Uptime below 95.0%: 50% credit.

Service credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures and must be claimed in writing within thirty (30) days of the affected month.

## 3. Support Response Times
Provider responds to support requests according to severity:

- Priority 1 (critical, service unavailable): within one (1) hour.
- Priority 2 (high, major feature impaired): within four (4) business hours.
- Priority 3 (normal): within one (1) business day.

## 4. Scheduled Maintenance
Routine maintenance is performed on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC. Provider gives at least forty-eight (48) hours' advance notice of any scheduled maintenance, which does not count against the uptime commitment.
