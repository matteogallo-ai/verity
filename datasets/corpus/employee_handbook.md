# Employee Handbook

This handbook summarizes the principal policies for employees of Acme Corporation. It is an internal document and does not form part of any customer agreement.

## Paid Time Off
Employees are entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing monthly. Up to five (5) unused days may be carried over into the following year.

## Working Model
Acme operates a hybrid working model. Employees must work from the office a minimum of two (2) days per week, with core collaboration hours between 10:00 and 16:00 local time.

## Probation
New employees serve a probationary period of three (3) months, during which either party may end the employment with one (1) week's notice.

## Notice of Resignation
After probation, employees are asked to give at least one (1) month's written notice of resignation.

## Business Expenses
Pre-approved business expenses are reimbursed within thirty (30) days of a complete expense claim.
