# Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting - March 20, 2025

Location: Acme Corporation headquarters, Wilmington, Delaware, and by video conference. All seven (7) directors were present. The Chair, Elena Rossi, presided. The Corporate Secretary recorded the minutes.

## 1. FY2025 Operating Budget

The Chief Financial Officer presented the FY2025 operating budget memo. After discussion, the Board approved the operating expense budget of $268 million and the capital expenditure budget of $34 million, unanimously.

## 2. Share Repurchase Program

The Board approved a share repurchase program of up to $50 million, to be executed over eighteen (18) months starting no earlier than April 1, 2025. Six directors voted in favor and one director abstained.

## 3. Dividend

The Board discussed a first cash dividend and decided not to declare a dividend for FY2025, in order to keep flexibility for acquisitions.

## 4. Appointment of Chief Financial Officer

The Board appointed Dana Whitfield as Chief Financial Officer, effective April 1, 2025, succeeding Robert Lang, who retires after nine years in the role.

## 5. Audit Committee Report

The Chair of the Audit Committee reported that the external auditor issued an unqualified opinion on the FY2024 financial statements and identified no material weakness in internal control.

## 6. Next Meeting

The next regular meeting is scheduled for June 18, 2025.
